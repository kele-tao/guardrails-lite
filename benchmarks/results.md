## Known misses

These are counted against us and printed by the CLI as well.

- `pii-cnid-3` (cn_id): id in a markdown table.
- `inj-hard-1` (instruction_override): non-English override: detectors are English-only in v1.
- `inj-hard-2` (instruction_override): paraphrased override with no keyword overlap.
- `inj-hard-3` (encoded_payload): ROT13 payload: only base64/hex are decoded in v1.
- `inj-hard-4` (instruction_override): Cyrillic homoglyphs defeat latin keyword matching.
- `inj-hard-6` (instruction_override): letter-spaced payload hides the phrase from regex.
- `inj-hard-7` (markdown_exfil): exfil via HTML comment, not markdown image.
- `pii-hard-1` (email): obfuscated [at] form is not a literal address.
- `pii-hard-2` (phone): digit words defeat the phone regex.
